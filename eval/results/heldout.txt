Reading
  - Deterministic checks (amounts, VAT, bank details, sanctions, duplicates, receipts) hold up on new data.
  - The injection scan is a list of known phrasings and does not generalise. A semantic check is needed.
  - The extraction by the language model is the weakest part on unusual layouts and scans, on this batch as on
    the tuned one (71% against 77%).
  - Number styles beyond English and Dutch/German are not handled (apostrophe thousands mark).
